**Public proposal by Juri Janovski, publishing as Juri Halveth.**

HALVETH invites xAI or another expressly authorized Musk-affiliated entity to discuss a staged research collaboration with a total program ceiling of **USD 40,000,000**.

The proposal is deliberately bounded. It does not sell or assign HALVETH, the complete research corpus, unpublished material, unprovided know-how, future work, names, marks, or all associated rights. The first contemplated exchange is one defined evaluation capsule under written terms.

## What is offered

- a 30-minute exploratory conversation;
- one bounded evaluation capsule after mutual agreement;
- declared purpose, scope, provenance, test conditions, evaluation criteria, and known limitations;
- later milestones only after their deliverables, acceptance tests, rights, funding, confidentiality, and publication rules are agreed in writing.

## What is not offered

- no sale of the complete HALVETH body of work;
- no implied production, commercial, training, sublicensing, transfer, or derivative-distribution right;
- no blockchain transaction or token approval;
- no claim that USD 40 million is already owed.
