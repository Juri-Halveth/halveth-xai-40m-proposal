## Proposed staged structure

1. **Evaluation:** Define one question and test one bounded capsule against agreed criteria.
2. **Validation:** Reproduce material behavior in an agreed environment and document gaps, risks, and useful capability.
3. **Development:** Build selected modules against written milestones and acceptance tests.
4. **Deployment or licensing:** Negotiate production rights, support, attribution, economics, and safeguards only for validated components.

The USD 40 million figure is the ceiling for the complete staged program. It is not the price of the initial capsule and it does not purchase the entire HALVETH body of work.
